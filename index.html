Sailing tips

  • The flag and wind dial show the wind. Sailing across it (a beam reach) is fastest.
  • You can't sail straight into the wind. Turn away until the dial turns green.
  • In a storm, lower your sails to protect the hull.
  • Gold in your hold is lost if you're shipwrecked. Dock at a port to bank it and save.
